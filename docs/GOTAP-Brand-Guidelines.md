The GOTAP Brand Guidelines provide a comprehensive overview of how to present the brand consistently and effectively across all touchpoints. This document is the foundational reference for everyone working with or representing GOTAP, ensuring the brand voice, visual identity, and values are communicated clearly and cohesively.

## 3. Brand Values & Tone of Voice

GOTAP communicates through four voice pillars. Every piece of copy, design, and interaction should reflect these consistently.

<tone_of_voice>
1. **Confident & Premium**: Speak with authority and restraint. Quality over volume.
2. **Modern & Professional**: Clean, current, and credible. No clutter, no gimmicks.
3. **Innovative & Forward Thinking**: Point toward what is next. Treat the future as already arriving.
4. **Sustainable & Conscious**: Build with responsibility and long-term thinking in mind.
</tone_of_voice>

## 4. Logo & Logotype

### 4.1 Concept

<logo_concept>
The name has two parts.

- **GO** is the call to action.
- **TAP** is the sound of the action.

The **O** moves through three stages: past, present, and future. Before, now, and after. It represents the shift a person feels the moment they use the technology.

**TAP** is the sound of contact. Fast and final. A signal that the future is already here.
</logo_concept>

### 4.3 Logo Usage Rules

<inferred_guidance>
*Inferred: reconstructed as standard practice, not verbatim from the deck. Confirm against the visual usage-rules slide before publishing.*

- **Clear space:** Maintain minimum clear space around the logo equal to the height of the "O" so it never crowds against other elements.
- **Minimum size:** Do not reproduce the logo so small that the wordmark loses legibility. Set a minimum print and digital size in the asset kit.
- **Color use:** Use the logo in solid black (#000000) on light backgrounds and solid white (#FFFFFF) on dark backgrounds to preserve maximum contrast.
- **Do not:** stretch or distort the mark, rotate it, change its colors outside the approved palette, add effects or shadows, recreate the wordmark in a different font, or place it on low-contrast or busy backgrounds.
</inferred_guidance>

## 5. Color Palette

### 5.1 Main Colors

The main colors represent GOTAP's core identity and establish a cohesive, recognizable brand presence. They anchor the complete brand, ensuring a premium, elegant look that reflects the values of innovation, simplicity, and connectivity across all touchpoints.

<main_colors>
| Color | HEX | RGB | Role |
|-------|-----|-----|------|
| Black | `#000000` | 0, 0, 0 | Primary brand color, backgrounds, type |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | 255, 255, 255 | Primary brand color, backgrounds, negative space |
</main_colors>

### 5.2 Accent Colors

The secondary colors add flexibility and depth to brand layouts. They organize content and set a mood without overpowering the core black and white.

<accent_colors>
| Color | HEX | RGB | Role |
|-------|-----|-----|------|
| Blue | `#0077B5` | 0, 119, 181 | Accent, links, professional/network cues |
| Gray | `#BBBBBB` | 187, 187, 187 | Accent, secondary text, dividers, subtle UI |
| Coral Red | `#F55C5B` | 245, 92, 91 | Accent, highlights, calls to action |
</accent_colors>

*Usage note: accents support the layout. Black and white remain dominant. Accents should never overpower the core palette.*

## 6. Typography

### 6.1 Primary Font: Michroma

Used for titles and display type.

<primary_font>
- **Font:** Michroma
- **Use:** Titles and headings
- **Sizes:**
  - Slide cover title: **54px**
  - Main title: **28px**
  - Secondary title: **14px**
- **Character set:** Full uppercase and lowercase Latin alphabet (Aa-Zz) and numerals 00-09.
</primary_font>

### 6.2 Secondary Font: Montserrat

Used for paragraphs and body copy.

<secondary_font>
- **Font:** Montserrat
- **Use:** Paragraphs and body text
- **Character set:** Full Latin alphabet (Aa-Zz), numerals 00-09, and symbols (`!@#$%^&*()`).
- **Available weights:** Thin, Extra Light, Light, Normal, Medium, Semi Bold, Bold, Extra Bold, Black.
- **Example in use:** "Your work on this project has been really impactful."
</secondary_font>
